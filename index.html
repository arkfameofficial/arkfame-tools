📑 PDF Studio

Merge files, rearrange pages, rotate, delete, split by range, stamp page numbers, export or raster-compress. Documents stay in your browser.

Use arrows to reorder, ↻ to rotate 90°, and ✓ to include/exclude. Page dimensions apply only in Flatten / compress mode; original-quality mode keeps original page sizes. Flatten/compress converts pages to images: text selection, searchable text, links and forms are lost; file size is not guaranteed to decrease. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.

🖼️ Image Lab

Batch resize, rotate, convert and export with dimensions and quality settings. JPG, PNG and WebP supported where your browser supports them.

Transparency becomes white when exporting to JPG. Resizing/re-encoding may remove EXIF metadata. Very large images can exceed browser memory.
